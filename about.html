ABOUT THE BUILDER

Designing the interface.
Thinking through the system.

I approach development as product problem-solving: understand the user, reduce friction, design the experience and build the technology that makes it reliable.

A developer mindset with product ownership.

My interest is not limited to writing a page that looks good. I enjoy connecting the visual layer with workflows, backend services, data and the operational reality behind a product.

That means asking practical questions: What happens after a button is pressed? Which service owns the request? How does the status update? What should the user see if something fails? These questions shape stronger products.
